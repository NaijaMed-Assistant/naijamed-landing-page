NaijaMed Assistant Limited (“NaijaMed”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the NaijaMed Assistant mobile application (“App”), an AI-enabled clinical support and patient triage platform for patients and healthcare professionals in Nigeria. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights.

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

a. Information you provide directly
- Full name
- Phone number
- Email address
- Password or account login details
- Profile details such as date of birth, gender, height, weight, blood group, allergies, home address, and medical history
- Symptoms, consultation information, chat messages, appointment requests, and notes submitted through the App
- For doctors: professional information, medical licence details, specialty and consultation activity.

b. Payment Card Information
Some features require a paid subscription. When you subscribe, we or our payment provider may collect payment card details, billing information, and transaction records to process payments and manage your subscription. We may receive limited card details, such as the card type and last four digits, along with payment confirmation and subscription status. Full card details may be processed by our payment provider and are not stored on our servers where the provider handles them directly.

c. Information collected automatically
- Device model, operating system, app version, and device identifiers
- App usage data, crash reports, diagnostics, performance logs, and error information
- IP address and internet connection data
- Notification preferences and engagement data



d. Health data
The App may process health-related information, including symptoms, medical history, treatment details, and consultation records. This is sensitive personal data and is handled with care.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Create and manage your account
- Provide symptom checking, triage support, consultation booking, and AI-assisted clinical workflows
- Allow patients and doctors to communicate within the App
- Prepare patient information for consultations and follow-up care
- Improve the App, fix bugs, and monitor performance
- Send important notifications, reminders, and account updates
- Maintain security, prevent fraud, and protect the platform
- Meet legal, regulatory, and safety requirements
- Support analytics, research, and product improvement

3. AI Clinical Support

The App may use artificial intelligence to ask symptom-related questions and provide general guidance to patients and doctors. This support is intended to assist users and clinicians, but it does not replace a doctor’s medical judgment or provide a diagnosis.

The AI-generated output may be incomplete, inaccurate, or not appropriate for every situation. You should not rely on it for emergency or life-threatening conditions. Always seek professional medical advice from a qualified healthcare provider when needed.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal data.

We may share your information only when necessary:
- With doctors or healthcare providers you consult through the App
- With trusted service providers that help us run the App, such as cloud hosting, analytics, push notification, crash reporting, email/SMS, security, and payment services
- With regulators, government authorities, or law enforcement when required by law
- When needed to protect life, health, safety, or legal rights
- In connection with a business sale, merger, or restructuring

Where third parties process your information, we require them to protect it and use it only for the services they provide to us.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data based on:
- Your consent
- The performance of services you request
- Compliance with legal obligations
- Our legitimate interests in running, securing, and improving the App
- Protection of health, safety, and legal rights

Because health information is sensitive, we obtain consent before collecting or using it where required by law and applicable standards.

6. Data Storage, Security, and Transfers

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including access controls, secure hosting, and encrypted transmission, to protect personal data. However, no digital system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your data may be stored on servers located outside Nigeria. Where this occurs, we take reasonable steps to ensure that your information is protected in line with applicable privacy standards and contractual safeguards.

7. Retention

We keep your data for as long as needed to:
- Provide the App and related services
- Maintain legal and regulatory records
- Resolve disputes and enforce agreements
- Protect user safety and service integrity

If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, unless we are required by law to retain certain information for longer, such as records needed for legal, medical, or regulatory reasons.

8. Your Rights

You may have the right to:
- Access personal data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
- Request deletion of your account and personal data
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
- Restrict or object to certain processing activities
- Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond in line with applicable law.

9. Deleting Your Account

You may delete your account by contacting us via email or call. If you request deletion, we will process it in accordance with this policy and applicable law.

10. Minors

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13, or the legal age of adulthood in your jurisdiction, without parental consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without such consent. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Emergency and Medical Disclaimer

NaijaMed Assistant does not provide emergency services yet. If you or someone else is in a medical emergency, call 112 or go to the nearest hospital immediately. Do not wait for a response from the App.
